# Accessibility & Contrast Standards

## Contrast & Legibility

- **Text Contrast**: All body and title text must maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against the card surface.
- **Arabic Text Clipping**: Maintain a line height of at least 1.3 to avoid truncating diacritics or descending Arabic glyphs.
- **Touch Targets**: All interactive elements (buttons, card taps, list items) MUST have a minimum touch target area of `44x44` dp.
- **Color Independence**: Never rely on color alone to convey status. Every status badge MUST combine text labels with a supporting icon.
