# Component Specification: Dialogs & Modals

- **Dialog Radius**: `20px` (`AppRadius.xl`).
- **Surface**: Pure surface background without heavy surface tinting.
- **Bottom Sheets**: Vertical top radius `20px`, draggable grab handle where scrollable content exists.
- **Actions**: Aligned to `MainAxisAlignment.end`, with Primary button on the natural action side (RTL end / LTR right).
