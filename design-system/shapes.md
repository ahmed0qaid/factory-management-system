# Shapes, Radius & Elevation Tokens

## Corner Radius System (`AppRadius`)

- **Small (`8px`)**: Badges, status pills, tags, chips.
- **Medium (`12px`)**: Sub-containers inside cards, form input fields, small buttons.
- **Large (`16px`)**: Standard HR Cards (`AppStatCard`, `AppActionCard`, list items).
- **Extra Large (`20–24px`)**: Dialogs, bottom sheets, header summary cards.

---

## Elevation & Shadows (`AppShadows`)

- **Flat Borders First**: Prefer `outlineVariant` borders over dark drop shadows.
- **Subtle Elevation**: If elevated, use `elevation: 1` with soft blur and low opacity (alpha 4–6%).
